# AI daily plan

**Diagnosis of XAUUSD Trading Performance:**

1. **Losing Hours:** The majority of losses occur between 05:00 and 07:00, indicating a potential issue with trading during these early hours.

2. **Win vs. Loss Shape:** The recent performance shows a mix of wins and losses, but losses tend to be larger than wins. For example, several losses exceed -1.5, while wins are mostly under +2.0, suggesting a negative risk-reward ratio.

3. **Net-Negative Strategy:** The strategy appears net-negative due to a higher frequency of significant losses compared to wins, leading to an overall loss in the trading stream.

**Recommendations:**

1. **Entry Threshold:** Implement a stricter entry threshold, such as only entering trades with a minimum expected move of 0.5% in the desired direction.

2. **Time Filter:** Avoid trading between 05:00 and 07:00, as this period shows a higher concentration of losses.

3. **SL/TP Settings:** Set a stop-loss (SL) at 1.5 times the average loss (around 1.5) and a take-profit (TP) at 2 times the average win (around 2.0) to improve the risk-reward ratio. 

These adjustments aim to enhance overall performance and reduce exposure during unfavorable trading hours.
